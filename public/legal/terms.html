8. Meeting people in real life

MapMeet does not run background checks, identity checks or any other vetting of its users. A verified email address is not a verified person. Ratings and reviews are written by other users and can be wrong, unfair or manipulated.

When you attend an event or meet someone you found through MapMeet, you do so entirely at your own risk. We are not a party to that meeting, we don't supervise it, and we are not responsible for the conduct of anyone you meet — online or off.

Basic precautions, which we strongly recommend:

  • Meet in a public place the first time.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you're going and who with.
  • Arrange your own transport, and keep your phone charged.
  • Don't share your home address, financial details or identity documents with someone you've just met.
  • Be sceptical of anyone who asks you for money, however sympathetic the story.
  • Trust your instincts and leave if something feels wrong.

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. Then report the user in the app so we can act on the account.
